GardenPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

GardenPress does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Your garden plans, frost dates, journal notes and harvest records are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage.

GardenPress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location is never requested – you choose your hardiness zone or frost dates yourself. Data leaves your device only when you print or save a backup file yourself.

Purchases of GardenPress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. GardenPress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting a plan (☰ More → Delete this plan) or by uninstalling the app.
