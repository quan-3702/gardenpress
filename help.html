🌱 How to use GardenPress

GardenPress helps you draw your vegetable garden, decide what goes where and know when to sow, plant and harvest. Everything stays on this computer – no account, no subscription.

Want to see an example first? ☰ More → Open a sample garden.

1. Set up your garden

Click ⚙ Garden settings. Enter the size of your garden in squares – one square is 1 foot (or 30 cm in metric) – and your frost dates. Pick your USDA hardiness zone to fill typical dates, or better, type the last spring frost and first fall frost for your town. Every date in the calendar is counted from these two dates.

2. Add beds

Add a raised bed, in-ground bed, container or path from the left panel. Drag a bed to move it and pull the green corner to resize it. Select a bed to rename it, type an exact size, duplicate or delete it. Ctrl+Z undoes the last change.

3. Plant

Pick a plant on the left, then click squares in a bed – or drag across several squares. The number on a square shows how many plants fit in it using square foot gardening (for example 16 carrots or 1 tomato). Large plants like squash and melons take 2 × 2 or 3 × 3 squares. Press Esc to stop planting. Use ⌫ Erase or right-click a square to remove a plant. Click a planted square to see spacing, dates, good neighbours and growing tips.

4. Check your garden

The button at the top shows good pairings and things to check. GardenPress warns you when two plants that don't grow well together are side by side (for example beans next to onions), when sweet corn is planted in too small a block, and when a crop may not have time to ripen before your first frost. Plants with a problem get a red mark on the layout.

5. Planting calendar

The 📅 Calendar tab lists every plant in your layout with bars for starting seeds indoors, planting out or sowing, sowing again (succession) and harvest, plus a to-do list for the next three weeks. Change the frost dates and the whole calendar moves.

6. Print

🖨 Print gives you the garden plan with a plant list and dates – take it outside while you plant. With Pro you can also print the planting calendar, plant labels for the garden and a seed and plant shopping list. In the print window choose Save as PDF to keep a file.

7. Next season and crop rotation (Pro)

☰ More → Start next season copies your beds into a new plan for next year. When you plant it, GardenPress warns you if a bed grows the same crop family as last year – rotating crops keeps soil healthy and pests down.

8. Journal and harvest log (Pro)

The 📓 Journal tab keeps dated notes and harvests. GardenPress adds up how much each crop gave you, so next year you know what to grow more of.

Backups

Save a backup now and then with ☰ More → Back up everything and restore it on another PC with Restore a backup.
